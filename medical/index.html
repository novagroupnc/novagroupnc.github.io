The operating thesis

From complex technology to supportable clinical products

The proposed business combines clinical strategy, medical silicon, regulated development and care operations in one accountable product pathway.

We own

Intended use, system architecture, evidence, quality, cybersecurity, workflow and post-market learning.

We partner

Foundry, assembly and test, specialty materials, sterilization, selected volume manufacturing, clinical sites and channels.

We avoid

Commodity devices, generic wellness applications, unmanaged data brokerage and capital-intensive manufacturing without a clinical case.

Proposed portfolio architecture

Shared platforms. Focused clinical use.

The portfolio remains subject to indication screening, feasibility review and board authorization. Each family shares core quality, regulatory, cybersecurity and manufacturing systems.

Proposed product family A

Implantable intelligence, built as a complete clinical platform

Implantable pressure, electrophysiology or biochemical sensing modules may connect secure telemetry with clinician-facing monitoring and procedure support. Initial indications would advance only after clinical actionability and manufacturing risk are reviewed together.

Stage-gated development

Progress follows evidence, not a fixed calendar

Each gate would require an approved evidence package, an accountable owner, a residual-risk decision, a capacity plan and explicit authorization to proceed, narrow or stop.

Gate 01

Concept

Unmet need, target outcome and feasibility screen.

Gate 02

Design input

Intended use, architecture and risk baseline.

Gate 03

Verification

Requirements, reliability and cyber evidence.

Gate 04

Clinical

Clinical evidence, usability and manufacturing release.

Gate 05

Market

Authorization, launch readiness, service and surveillance.

First 18-month objective

Build the clinical, technical and operating foundation for initial product programs. The first phase focuses on evidence, accountable teams and a credible path to pilot development.

Manufacturing operating model

Control the work that protects patients

The proposed model retains product architecture, design assurance, supplier quality and critical release decisions while qualifying specialist partners for capital-intensive processes.

Make-versus-buy decisions would consider patient risk, differentiation, evidence ownership, supply resilience and economics.
